# Downtown Duel → open-world creature RPG — build brief

You are continuing an existing **Godot 4.7.2 / GDScript** project (this repo). Read `CLAUDE.md` first: it
describes the generator-driven world (`tools/gen_world.py` writes `scenes/arena.tscn`), combat, the RPG layer
(levels, coins, gear, shops), monster dialogue (spare / befriend / followers), touch controls and the web export.
**Build on it. Do not restart, switch engines, or rewrite working systems without a reason you write down.**

## Vision (one paragraph)
A souls-like, open-world creature RPG. You explore a huge hand-plus-procedurally built land of several cities and
wild regions, befriend *any* creature through treats and kindness (no capture balls), and fight alongside a party of
creature companions in real-time lock-on combat. Gather and mine resources, craft treats and gear, and heal and save
at a Sanctuary in each city. Inspirations: **Palworld** (creatures that fight and work beside you, alpha bosses),
**Pokémon** (party, types, healing centres), **The Witcher 3** (big regions, cities, contract boards, signposts),
**Dark Souls / Sekiro** (stamina, punishing telegraphed fights, checkpoints, dropped currency) and **Minecraft**
(block mining in mines and quarries).

## Hard constraints
1. **Engine:** Godot 4.7.2, GDScript, **Compatibility renderer** (it is the only one that exports to the web).
   Desktop (Windows) build = the full game. Web build = a playable slice (one region) that must keep working.
2. **Legal / IP:** only CC0 or MIT assets and code. No Pokémon, Palworld or Nintendo names, designs, sounds or UI.
   Mechanics are fine; **don't** use throw-an-item-to-capture or "ball" capture. Befriending is via treats, bond and
   dialogue, which is what this game wants anyway. No Sketchfab or Mixamo downloads (mixed or login-gated licences).
   **Do not use the PMD Sprite Repository (sprites.pmdcollab.org)**: it is official DS Pokémon Mystery Dungeon rips plus
   fan art of Pokémon, so Nintendo owns the characters regardless of contributor permission. It may be used only as
   *reference* for expressive 2D portraits (emotion faces for dialogue); draw or generate original ones.
3. **Data-driven:** creatures, moves, items, recipes, treats, quests and cities live in data files
   (GDScript `const` dicts or `.json` under `data/`), not hard-coded in logic.
4. **Generated world:** keep `scenes/*.tscn` world content generated by Python tools in `tools/`. Hand-authored
   pieces (a city layout, a dungeon) are generator *inputs* (data), not hand-edited `.tscn`.
5. **Performance budget (desktop):** 60 fps on a laptop RTX 4060 at 1080p. **Web:** 30 fps on a mid laptop,
   ≤ 120 MB download. Use MultiMesh for vegetation and rocks, `visibility_range` LODs, and streamed world cells.
6. **Tests:** every milestone adds headless SceneTree tests under `tests/` (`godot --headless -s res://tests/x.gd`)
   that print PASS/FAIL. Touch tests need xvfb (see `CLAUDE.md`). Run every test before each commit.
7. **Commits:** one commit per finished milestone (and smaller ones inside it), with a clear message. Update
   `README.md`, `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/PROGRESS.md` (what's done, what's next, known issues) every milestone.
8. Keep the existing controls working (keyboard + mouse, and touch on phones), and add new actions to both.

## World scale (be honest about "massive")
- **Target:** a **2 km × 2 km** world (4 km², a little smaller than Skyrim's playable core) split into
  **128 m cells** that stream in and out around the player. Architect it so a second 2 × 2 km region can be added later.
- Terrain: **Terrain3D** (MIT, github.com/TokisanGames/Terrain3D). It supports the Compatibility renderer in 1.0+;
  its web export is *experimental*, so verify the web build early. If web fails, the web slice uses the current flat
  generated arena, and Terrain3D stays desktop-only behind a feature flag.
- Regions (each with a city, wild zones, a mine or cave, 3–5 points of interest and one Alpha boss):
  | Region | City (kit) | Biome / wilds | Mine / dungeon |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | **Bakulia (home)** | **Haldwani bazaar** + lake town in the hills + the Estate (see below) | Terai fields, sal forest, Gola riverbed | river quarry + quarry pit |
  | Heartland | **Oakvale** — Medieval Village MegaKit | meadows, farms (LowPoly Farm Buildings) | copper quarry |
  | Old Metro | **Downtown** — Downtown City MegaKit (existing) | overgrown streets, rooftops | sewer tunnels |
  | Mossreach Wilds | **Mossreach** camp — Fantasy Props + Stylized Nature | dense forest, rivers | crystal cave |
  | Helix Coast | **Helix Outpost** — Modular Sci-Fi MegaKit | cliffs, ruins | research mine |
  | Gravehold | **Gravehold** citadel — LowPoly Modular Dungeon + KayKit Dungeon | dead forest | catacombs (endgame) |
- Witcher-style signposts (fast travel between discovered Sanctuaries), roads between cities, day/night cycle.

### Home region: Bakulia & the Kumaon foothills (the owner's real home turf, Haldwani, Uttarakhand)
The starting region is a **stylised, compressed recreation of the Haldwani area**, built around the owner's family
estate at Bakulia. It is the player's **home base** (Palworld-style): befriended creatures live and work here.
- **Geography (compress ~25 km of the real area into the 2 km region; keep the feel, not 1:1 scale):**
  - South: flat **Terai plains**, with wheat, sugarcane and mustard fields, mango and litchi orchards, village lanes,
    and a growing colony of houses at the edge of the estate.
  - Through the middle: the **Gola river**, a wide braided gravel riverbed with stone crushers and sand quarries.
    This is the **main overworld quarry and mining area**: gravel, stone, river crystals, and a quarry pit with
    GridMap block mining.
  - East and west: **sal forest** with a new four-lane highway being cut along its edge (construction props, cones,
    survey stakes): wild creatures and an Alpha live here.
  - North: the land rises into the **Kumaon hills** (pine forest, switchback road through a Kathgodam-style gate)
    up to a **lake town in the hills** (a Nainital-inspired lakeside city with a boat pier and its own Sanctuary).
  - The **Haldwani bazaar** town sits between the estate and the hills, with dense two-storey shops (Medieval kit
    plus city kit), a market street, a bus stand and the region's first Sanctuary.
- **The Estate (home base), a long, narrow farm plot:** starts as a modest farmhouse and fields. The player
  **upgrades it with crafted materials** into the owner's dream compound, one upgrade at a time: a **bamboo grove**
  border, a **horse paddock and stable**, a workshop (3D printers, workbench, anvil), a **GPU server room** whose
  waste heat warms a **mushroom room** (mushrooms are a treat ingredient), poly-houses (berries and herbs for treats),
  a small **pool**, a gaming room, and finally a rooftop **observatory with a telescope** (night-time event: rare
  Gale and Shade creatures appear when you use it). Creatures assigned to the estate farm, mine the riverbed and
  craft automatically (Palworld base work) and feed resources into the treat chain.
- **Atmosphere:** warm, hazy North-Indian light; winter fog in the mornings; dhaba stalls; chai cups as a
  stamina item; Kumaoni-inspired music (CC0 / commissioned later). Signs in English + Devanagari.
- **Privacy:** this is a public game. Do **not** use real coordinates, plot boundaries, survey numbers or real
  people's names. Use public terrain data only for the general landscape shape (SRTM elevation is public domain;
  OpenStreetMap roads and rivers need an "© OpenStreetMap contributors" credit), then stylise it.

## Systems to build — in this order (MVP first, each playable before the next)

### M0 — Save / load (prerequisite)
`FileAccess` to `user://save.json` (this is IndexedDB on the web). Save player stats, inventory, party, befriended
storage, world flags, discovered Sanctuaries. Autosave on resting at a Sanctuary and every 2 minutes.

### M1 — Party & companion combat (the core of the game)
- **Befriend any creature** (bosses only through special treats, see M2). Current follow-only friends become real party members.
- **Party:** 3 active companions + unlimited storage at Sanctuaries. Swap at a Sanctuary.
- **Companion AI** (extend `enemy.gd` or split a `companion.gd`): follow at formation offsets; engage the player's
  lock-on target (or the nearest enemy attacking the player); use the same telegraph → lunge → recover attack
  pattern; respect attack tokens (enemies may target companions too). Commands on a radial / D-pad:
  **Attack my target · Follow · Hold position · Return**.
- Companion stats: level, XP (shared with the player), HP, attack, 2 moves from its element.
- **Elements:** Ember, Tide, Verdant, Stone, Gale, Shade (6) with a 1.5× / 0.67× wheel. Every creature in
  `assets/monsters` gets an element and a base-stat line in `data/creatures`.
- **Knocked out:** a companion at 0 HP is "exhausted", returns to your side as a ghost and recovers at a Sanctuary or with a potion.
- HUD: party portraits with HP bars, command prompt, element icons.

### M2 — Treats, bond and befriending
- **Bond meter** per wild creature (0–100). Petting, gifts, sparing, a favourite treat and fighting beside it raise
  it. At the threshold it asks to join. Hostile creatures must be weakened or calmed first (existing dialogue).
- **Treats are crafted** (M4) from gathered ingredients: berries, honey, mushrooms, fish, plus **rare drops from
  Alpha beasts** (e.g. "Emberwing Scale"). Tiers: Simple → Hearty → Royal → Mythic.
  Each creature has a favourite flavour (×2 bond). **Alphas and bosses only accept Mythic treats**, which need
  materials from other Alphas, so the treat chain is how you progress.
- Rare **"Lustre" variants** (1 in 200 spawn, recoloured, +10 % stats).
- **Alpha creatures** (Palworld-style): one per region, larger scale, crown icon, a phase-2 move set, unique drop.

### M3 — Gathering & Minecraft-style mining
- **Overworld:** resource nodes (trees, rocks, ore veins, crystals, berry bushes, herbs) as MultiMesh + collider
  pools. They deplete and respawn on a timer. Hit with a tool (new `use_tool` action) → drops.
- **Mines, quarries and caves:** real **block mining** in a `GridMap` (1 m cubes) inside each mine zone. Break blocks
  (with an animation and particles), place blocks, and ore blocks by depth (stone → copper → iron → silver → gold → crystal).
  Use a generated noise-based layout per mine. Persist removed and placed cells in the save.
- **Tool tiers** (wood → stone → copper → iron → crystal pickaxe / axe) gate ore tiers.
- **Creature work skills** (Palworld): companions with *Mining*, *Lumber* or *Gathering* skills auto-harvest nearby
  nodes when told to "Work" near them, and can work at a home camp later.

### M4 — Inventory & crafting
Grid inventory (stacking, weight-free), workbench / cooking pot / anvil stations in cities and camps,
data-driven recipes (`data/recipes`): treats, potions, tools, gear upgrades. UI must work with touch.

### M4.5 — The Estate (home base)
Build the Bakulia estate as an upgradeable base: a data file of upgrade tiers (farmhouse → bamboo border →
stable → workshop → GPU room + mushroom room → poly-houses → pool → observatory), each with a material cost and
placed from kit pieces. Assign befriended creatures to jobs (farm, mine riverbed, lumber, craft) and collect
their output from a storage chest. Keep it small and readable first (5 upgrades), then add more.

### M5 — Cities & Sanctuaries (healing centres)
- **City generator** (extend `gen_world.py`): a data file per city (districts, plaza, gates, walls, roads) → builds
  from kit modules. Reuse and extend the existing `house()` builder: 1–2 storeys, balconies, chimneys, shutters,
  towers (`Roof_Tower_RoundTiles`), market stalls, lamp posts (`Lantern_Wall`, `Torch_Metal`), walls and gates
  (dungeon wall pieces), fences, vines (`Prop_Vine*`), crates and barrels. NPCs: Universal Base Characters with
  Modular Outfits, plus KayKit Adventurers for guards and travellers, with idle, talk and walk loops on simple paths.
- **Sanctuary** (one per city, recognisable from far away by the same silhouette everywhere):
  - Building: a 10 × 12 m Medieval-kit hall (2 storeys, `Wall_Plaster_*`, round door, `Roof_RoundTiles_8x12`) with
    a tower and a **glowing crystal or lantern beacon** on top (emissive material + OmniLight). Banners
    (`Banner_1_Cloth`) in a Sanctuary colour (teal) on both sides of the door.
  - Inside or around: beds (`Bed_Twin1/2`), a cauldron and potions (`Cauldron`, `Potion_*`, `Shelf_Small_Bottles`),
    bookcase, candles; a **Healer NPC** (Universal Base Character, white/teal outfit, `Spell_Simple_Idle` loop); a
    **Creature Ledger** (party storage terminal) — a lectern (`BookStand`) in medieval cities, `Prop_Computer` in Helix.
  - Function: full heal for the player and party (with a sound and particles), **rest = checkpoint** (souls-like:
    resting respawns non-boss enemies), sets the respawn point, opens fast travel, swaps the party, autosaves.
  - Variants per kit: Downtown = a converted brick clinic with the same beacon; Helix = a sci-fi med-bay with a
    holo-cross; Gravehold = a candle-lit chapel.
- **Contract board** in each city (Witcher-style): data-driven hunts, befriending and delivery quests.

### M6 — Souls-like depth
Stamina (attacks, dodge, sprint), heavy attack (hold), **parry / perfect-dodge window** (slow-mo plus a counter),
poise and stagger on enemies, boss phases, **drop coins on death and recover them from your bloodstain**,
"rest" at Sanctuaries respawns the world, harder and readable telegraphs. Companions can be revived mid-fight with an item.

### M7 — Massive world pass
Terrain3D landscape for the 2 × 2 km world, cell streaming (`ResourceLoader.load_threaded_request`), MultiMesh
foliage, HLOD impostors for far cities, roads, rivers (plane + shader), day/night plus weather, region music
(CC0 / OpenGameArt), a world map (extend `minimap.gd`) with discovered Sanctuaries and signposts.

### M8 — Polish & release
Main menu, settings (sensitivity, graphics preset, touch layout), a tutorial in Oakvale, a credits screen for all
CC0 packs, a trailer-ready intro flyover across the regions, a web slice export and a Windows export, and an itch.io page text.

## How to work
1. Start by reading `CLAUDE.md`, `README.md`, `tools/gen_world.py`, `scripts/enemy.gd`, `scripts/player.gd` and `scripts/items.gd`.
2. Write `docs/PLAN.md`: for each milestone, list the files, data schemas, tests and risks. Then build **M0 → M8 in
   order**. Don't start a milestone until the previous one passes its tests and the web export still runs.
3. After each milestone: run all tests, export Web (`godot --headless --export-release "Web" export/web/index.html`)
   and confirm it loads, update the docs, commit, and append a short changelog to `docs/PROGRESS.md`.
4. Flag over-engineering: prefer the simplest version that plays well, then iterate.
5. When something is ambiguous, choose the option that keeps the game fun to play in the browser and on a phone, and write the decision in `docs/PROGRESS.md`.
