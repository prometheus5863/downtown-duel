## Description (paste into the main description box)

**Make friends. Fight together. Explore everything.**

Downtown Duel is a small open-world creature adventure that runs right in your browser. There are no capture
balls here: you win creatures over the old-fashioned way, with a scratch behind the ears, the right treat and a
little patience. Then they fight beside you.

**What you'll do**
- **Befriend any creature.** Pet them, feed them treats, spare them in a fight. Every creature has a favourite
  flavour that counts double, and once their bond is high enough they'll ask to join you.
- **Fight as a team.** Lead a party of three companions in real-time, lock-on combat. Order them to attack, follow,
  hold or gather. Six elements beat each other in a circle, so pick your team well.
- **Take on the Alphas.** Four crowned bosses rule the wilds. They hit harder once they're angry, and only a
  Mythic treat will ever win one over.
- **Explore ten connected zones.** A city downtown, a medieval village, a forest grove, ruins swallowed by ivy, a
  sci-fi bazaar, a skyport and more, linked by roads that loop the whole map.
- **Mine and gather.** Dig into a hill of blocks where ores get richer the deeper you go, chop trees and pick
  berries. Your companions can do the work for you.
- **Gear up.** Eight armors that change how your hero looks, weapons, charms and better tools from three shops.
- **Your progress saves** in your browser automatically.

**Controls**
Keyboard + mouse: WASD to move, click to look around · J / left click to strike · K / right click for ember ·
Space to dodge · Tab to lock on · F to talk and shop · 1-5 for party orders · C for your character ·
**H shows every control.**
Phone / tablet (hold it sideways): left thumb moves, drag on the right to look, buttons for everything else, **?**
shows every control.

**Tips**
- Watch for the red **!** — a creature is winding up. Dodge, then hit back.
- Stuck with a grumpy creature? Weaken it, or calm it with a treat first.
- Rest at the Creature Ledger in Oakvale to heal your whole party.

**Credits**
Made with Godot 4.7. 3D art by Quaternius (CC0): Universal Base Characters, Modular Character Outfits, Ultimate
Monsters, Universal Animation Library and the City, Medieval Village, Fantasy Props, Sci-Fi and Stylized Nature
MegaKits. Block textures from the Kenney Voxel Pack (CC0).

**Known limits**
First load is about 86 MB (your browser keeps it afterwards). Saves live in the browser you play in, so a different
browser or a private window starts a fresh game.
